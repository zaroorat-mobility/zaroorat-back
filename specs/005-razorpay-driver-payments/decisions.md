### UD-1 — How may Finance resolve a mismatched payment? (was DR-1)

- **Question**: when Razorpay captured an amount or currency different from the intent, may Finance "CREDIT" it, and for how much?
- **Options**:
  - (a) CREDIT only when captured ≥ intent amount and currency is INR; credit exactly the intent amount; refund the excess. Otherwise REFUND the full captured amount.
  - (b) REFUND only, never credit.
  - (c) Credit the captured amount, whatever it is.
- **Consequences**:
  - (a) Drivers who overpaid still get their purchase; underpayments are returned. Needs excess-refund handling.
  - (b) Simplest and safest; the driver must pay again; more support load.
  - (c) Changes what a recharge or plan costs, which is a new pricing policy.
- **Recommendation**: (a).
- **Constraint found in review**: for `MULTIPLE_CAPTURES` (A7), the intent already has a successful payment. The one-success-per-intent index (data-model §3) therefore makes CREDIT to the **same** intent impossible. Under every option, A7 resolves as REFUND unless Finance also approves "credit as a new recharge", which would need a new intent. That would be a new policy and is not assumed.
- **Decision-maker**: Finance (with owner).
- **Blocks**: T081, T082. Review cases still open safely without it, because no money moves.

### UD-2 — Ledger treatment of captured-but-unapplied money (was TD-14)

- **Question**: where does money Razorpay captured, but we did not apply, sit in the books?
- **Options**:
  - (a) A new liability account `PAYMENT_SUSPENSE` against `GATEWAY_CLEARING`, released by CREDIT or REFUND.
  - (b) Leave it off the ledger until resolved.
  - (c) Post it to the purpose account immediately.
- **Consequences**:
  - (a) The ledger matches what Razorpay holds, and refunds use the existing lifecycle.
  - (b) Ledger and provider disagree; settlement reconciliation breaks.
  - (c) Recognises wallet credit or revenue that was not earned.
- **Recommendation**: (a). The account name follows the architecture spec §10.4 proposal `SUSPENSE:unmatched_psp`.
- **Also required in the same decision**: the ledger legs for **refunding** held money. Under (a) they are sourced from `PAYMENT_SUSPENSE`. Under (b) or (c), Finance must say which accounts the refund reserve, settle and fail legs post to, because the existing refund lifecycle assumes the payment was applied.
- **Decision-maker**: Finance / auditor.
- **Blocks**: the suspense posting in T024, and T081 for both CREDIT and REFUND. Until approved, T024 opens the review case and records a `CAPTURED_REVIEW` row **without** a ledger posting, the gap is reported in reconciliation, and no held payment can be resolved.

### R1 — Order mismatch rejected before the gate

- **Decision.**
  - A payment is evaluated only against the intent that owns its `order_id`.
  - On `verify`, a fetched payment whose `order_id` differs from the stored order is answered `400 PAYMENT_ORDER_MISMATCH` before the gate: no state change, a security metric.
  - The claiming intent never moves to MANUAL_REVIEW, and no hold row is written on it.
  - A payment for an order that none of our intents owns is A9 `UNKNOWN_ORDER` (case, no intent).
- **Rationale.** A hold row on the wrong intent would occupy INV-2's unique index and block the owner's real application. Moving the claiming intent to review would hold that driver's own later payment (R1 in phase2-risk-review.md).
- **Consequences.**
  - The Phase 2 gate code moves the claiming intent to `MANUAL_REVIEW` on `ORDER_MISMATCH`; R1-T changes that.
  - The Razorpay webhook's intent-UUID fallback in `findByGatewayReference` is removed in R1-T.
  - T043 must not ship before R1-T.
- **Text to align.**
  - data-model.md §3 ("every review except A6 writes a hold row") gets an exception for ORDER_MISMATCH.
  - spec.md rules table A5 gets a note: ORDER_MISMATCH on `verify` follows the failure table.
  - data-model.md §2 drops ORDER_MISMATCH from the moves into MANUAL_REVIEW.

### R4 — `UNUSABLE_PAYLOAD`

- **Decision.**
  - A signed webhook whose payment entity lacks a well-formed `id`, `order_id`, `amount`, `currency` or `status` (or `amount_paid` on `order.paid`) is stored with the terminal outcome `UNUSABLE_PAYLOAD`, `processed = true`, and a sanitized reason, and raises an alert.
  - It is answered 200; it is not retried.
  - The payment is settled only from Razorpay's own records (reconciliation, sweep). Nothing from that payload moves money.
- **Rationale.** The same malformed payload repeats on every retry. Answering 5xx would only park it and risk Razorpay disabling the webhook. `MISMATCH` and `NO_ACTION` would hide a defect.
- **Consequences.**
  - Needs the `gateway_events.outcome` column (T019c).
  - T061 must scan FAILED intents and T067 must exist, so that every such payment is recovered.
  - T099 alerts on the outcome.
- **Text to align.** data-model.md §4 adds `UNUSABLE_PAYLOAD` to the terminal outcomes.

### R6a, R6b, R6c — Subscription purchase safeguards

**R6a.**

- **Decision.** `purchase` answers `409 PAYMENT_UNDER_REVIEW` while the pending subscription's intent is MANUAL_REVIEW.
- **Rationale.** Prevents a second payment while the first is held.

**R6b.**

- **Decision.** `REUSE` only when the requested plan equals the pending subscription's plan. Otherwise apply TD-11:
  1. `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on the old intent;
  2. cancel it (only from PENDING, FAILED or EXPIRED; never from SUCCEEDED);
  3. create the new intent.

  The explicit protection against late captures: a capture on the CANCELLED intent is A8 `UNMATCHED_CAPTURE`, a review case with no revenue and no activation (D-6 governs its refund).

- **Rationale.** Today a late capture on a superseded order books revenue with no activation and dead-letters the activation event (phase2-risk-review.md R6).

**R6c.**

- **Decision.** No payment-starting path opens while the switch is OFF. The driver sees an existing intent through `GET /payments/intents/:id` (T043).

**Together.**

- **Consequences.** `payments.subscription_checkout` stays OFF until T051–T056 are done. R6a needs a new error code and an app message (T055/T056 area).
- **Text to align.** tasks.md T051/T052 add R6a and the plan-match rule. spec.md FR-005 notes R6a.

### TD-3 — No receipt-based recovery

- **Decision.**
  - The receipt (`zr1_` + 32 hex) stays, for traceability and the length limit only; it is never used to find or reuse an order.
  - A crash between Razorpay creating an order and our row being written leaves an **orphan unpaid order**. It is accepted and documented: no payment can be made on it, because the app only ever receives an order id from our response.
  - The idempotency-key lookup before every provider call (implemented) prevents our own duplicates.
  - Provider-supported order idempotency is investigated through T008.
- **Rationale.** Sandbox S2c/S2d: test mode accepted a duplicate receipt and indexed it about 2 minutes later.
- **Consequences.**
  - The four T013 receipt-recovery cases stay `todo` with the TD-3 reason. They are removed or rewritten only if a provider mechanism is adopted later.
  - The orphan count is visible in Razorpay's order list, not in our database.
- **Text to align.** spec.md FR-002 status "under review" becomes "decided 2026-10-21 (orphan accepted)".

## 3. Constitution amendment AM-1 (text approved under UD-3 on 2026-10-20; not yet written into the constitution, task T001)

Current §9.4: "Webhook verification — signature, timestamp tolerance, missing-event-id rejection, replay detection — is a trust boundary and is not weakened."

Proposed §9.4: "Webhook verification — signature over the raw body, missing-event-id rejection, durable replay detection, and a freshness rule — is a trust boundary and is not weakened. The freshness rule is **provider-specific**:

- where the provider signs a delivery timestamp (Stripe `t=`), that signed timestamp is checked against a short tolerance;
- where it does not (Razorpay), the maximum accepted age MUST be at least the provider's documented retry horizon (24 h for Razorpay), and deduplication runs before any age check.

A valid signature proves origin only. It never proves which driver or payment intent a payment belongs to."

Rationale: [research.md R-4](./research.md#r-4-webhook-freshness-vs-the-constitution). The constitution version moves 1.0.0 → 1.1.0.
