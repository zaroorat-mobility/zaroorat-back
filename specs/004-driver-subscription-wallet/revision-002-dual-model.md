# Revision 2: Dual Driver Payment Model (Subscription vs. Commission)

**Feature**: `004-driver-subscription-wallet` | **Revision date**: 2026-09-08 | **Supersedes**: the single-model design in `spec.md`/`data-model.md`/`decisions.md`/`plan.md` as they stood before this revision (git history/prior conversation turn preserves the original for reference; the files themselves are now updated in place, since nothing has been implemented yet — constitution §3.2's "additive, forward-only" migration rule applies to _deployed_ schema, not to a pre-implementation spec revision).

This document is sections 1–15 of the requested review. Section 16 ("final clean specification") is **not** duplicated here — it _is_ the updated `spec.md`, `data-model.md`, `decisions.md`, and `plan.md`, which this document references by section rather than restating, to keep one authoritative copy of each rule.

> **Note (later revision)**: this document's BD-1 discussion (§1, §2 item 7, §9, §11, §12 item 16) describes a "cap the deduction at current balance, record the shortfall via `expectedAmount`" mechanism that was accurate _at the time this document was written_ but has since been **withdrawn entirely** by a later correction. There is no cap, no partial deduction, and no shortfall record in the current design — an insufficient wallet balance at completion is now an exceptional, logged invariant violation with no wallet write at all. `expectedAmount` no longer exists as a field. See `decisions.md` BD-1 (as it now reads) for the authoritative, current rule; this file is left as the historical record of the dual-model revision's own reasoning and is not rewritten to match.

## 2. Business Rule Changes (old → new)

| #   | OLD rule                                                                                                 | NEW rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Every driver has both a subscription (access) and a commission wallet (ride cost)                        | A driver has **exactly one** payment model at a time: `SUBSCRIPTION` or `COMMISSION`. The two mechanisms are mutually exclusive per ride.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 2   | Subscription plans: Weekly, Monthly                                                                      | Subscription plans: **1-Day, Weekly, Monthly** (company-configurable, not a fixed enum of exactly these three — see `data-model.md`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 3   | Active subscription is required for **every** driver to accept a ride (FR-004)                           | Active subscription is required **only** for a `SUBSCRIPTION`-model driver. A `COMMISSION`-model driver's eligibility is governed solely by wallet balance, with **no** subscription requirement.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 4   | Ride acceptance **reserves/locks** the expected commission against the wallet (FR-018)                   | Ride acceptance performs a **read-only check** (`balance >= expectedCommission`); nothing is locked or set aside.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 5   | Wallet has an `available` balance and a separately tracked `locked`/reserved balance (FR-011)            | Wallet has a **single balance** figure. No locked/reserved balance exists.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 6   | Ride completion **releases the reservation** and deducts (FR-022)                                        | Ride completion **deducts directly** from the current balance under a row lock — no release step, because nothing was reserved.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 7   | Final commission exceeding the amount **reserved at acceptance** is capped at the reserved amount (BD-1) | Final commission exceeding the wallet's **currently available balance at completion** is capped at that available balance. The _policy_ (never negative, cap and record the shortfall) is unchanged — only what it's capped _against_ changes, because "reserved amount" no longer exists.                                                                                                                              |
| 8   | Concurrent ride-acceptance race is resolved by locking the wallet row **at reservation time** (FR-019)   | The race is structurally moot: a driver can only ever have one active ride at a time (a pre-existing, unrelated system invariant), so at most one ride per driver can ever be mid-flight consuming commission at once. The wallet row is still locked, but **at deduction time**, not at accept time, and purely as ordinary transaction safety, not as a race-prevention mechanism this feature must invent (see §10). |
| 9   | Every completed ride runs the same commission-deduction logic                                            | Ride completion **branches on the ride's payment model**: `SUBSCRIPTION` rides never touch the wallet; `COMMISSION` rides always do.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 10  | Wallet recharge amount is any positive number the driver types                                           | Wallet recharge amount is **either** a company-configured predefined amount ("box") **or** a custom amount within a company-configured min/max range.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 11  | (not previously addressed)                                                                               | Company defines a **Commission Plan** (how commission is calculated for commission-model drivers), distinct from Subscription Plan and from the Wallet itself — see §3.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 12  | (not previously addressed)                                                                               | A driver can **change payment model**; the change is staged and takes effect at a well-defined boundary, never retroactively, and never splits a single ride across two models (§9, §12).                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

## 3. Updated Business Model

### 3.1 Subscription-based driver workflow

1. Driver selects `paymentModel = SUBSCRIPTION` (first-time choice, or switching from `COMMISSION` — see §9).
2. Driver selects an active `SubscriptionPlan` (1-Day / Weekly / Monthly — company-configured).
3. Driver pays; **only** payment-provider confirmation activates the subscription (never a client-reported success).
4. While `DriverSubscription.status = ACTIVE` and `expiryDate > now`, the driver is eligible for **new** rides. No wallet balance check occurs anywhere in this driver's ride-acceptance or ride-completion path.
5. Every ride this driver completes while under this model produces **no** commission-wallet transaction and **no** commission-wallet ledger entry. The subscription fee already paid is the entire commission-collection mechanism for this driver's rides during the active period — recognized as `SUBSCRIPTION_REVENUE` once, at payment time, not per ride.
6. If the subscription expires before a new ride, the driver is not offered new rides. If it expires while a ride is already in progress, that ride completes exactly as normal (BD-2, unchanged).

### 3.2 Commission-based driver workflow

1. Driver selects `paymentModel = COMMISSION` (first-time choice, or switching from `SUBSCRIPTION` — see §9).
2. Driver's `DriverCommissionWallet` is created lazily on first use (unchanged from the original design).
3. Driver recharges the wallet — either a predefined amount from the company's configured `WalletRechargeOption` list, or a custom amount within the configured min/max — **only** payment-provider confirmation credits the wallet.
4. Before each ride is assigned/accepted, the backend computes the expected commission (via the applicable `CommissionPlan`) and checks `wallet.balance >= expectedCommission`. Sufficient → eligible, ride proceeds, **nothing is locked**. Insufficient → the ride is not assigned/accepted for this driver.
5. No subscription of any kind is required for this driver to be eligible — the wallet check is the entire eligibility mechanism for the commission dimension (all other, unrelated eligibility gates — verification, suspension, vehicle, online status — are unchanged and still apply, exactly as they do for a subscription-model driver).
6. At ride completion, the backend computes the final commission, deducts `min(finalCommission, currentBalance)` from the wallet under a row lock, writes one immutable wallet transaction, and posts one balanced ledger group crediting the company's commission account. If `finalCommission > currentBalance`, the wallet is capped at zero-or-current-balance (never negative through this path) and the shortfall is recorded for reporting, never charged to the driver as a debt (BD-1, revised mechanism, same policy).

### 3.3 Wallet workflow (recharge)

```text
Driver opens Wallet
        ↓
Enters custom amount  OR  selects a predefined WalletRechargeOption
        ↓
Backend validates: amount > 0, within [min, max] (custom) or is an active option (predefined)
        ↓
Clicks Pay → PaymentIntent created (purpose = DRIVER_COMMISSION_RECHARGE), gateway call OUTSIDE any DB transaction
        ↓
Driver completes payment with the provider
        ↓
Provider confirms (webhook, signature-verified + deduplicated — OR authenticated confirm call that itself calls the gateway)
        ↓
Backend applyConfirmation (DB-only transaction): ledger group posted, wallet credited, transaction row written
        ↓
Wallet balance increases by exactly the confirmed amount, exactly once
```

The wallet is **never** credited by the "Clicks Pay" step itself — that step only creates a `CREATED`-status `PaymentIntent`. Crediting happens exclusively inside the provider-confirmation-triggered transaction, satisfying "must not be credited merely because the driver clicked Pay" by construction (this was already true in the original design's `IntentService.applyConfirmation` pattern, `research.md §1.2`, and is unchanged by this revision).

## 4. Updated End-to-End Workflow

### 4.1 Driver selects payment model

`POST /api/v1/drivers/payment-model { paymentModel: 'SUBSCRIPTION' | 'COMMISSION' }` (new — see §7). First-time selection applies immediately (nothing to preserve yet). A **change** from an existing model is staged per §9's rules, never applied retroactively to an in-progress ride.

### 4.2 Subscription flow

Unchanged in mechanism from the original design (`plan.md` "Driver Subscription Design"): `PENDING_PAYMENT` → intent created (purpose `DRIVER_SUBSCRIPTION_PAYMENT`) → provider confirms → `SUBSCRIPTION_REVENUE` ledger credit → durable outbox event → subscription activated (`ACTIVE`). Only change: `billingPeriod` now includes `DAILY`, and activation additionally sets `Driver.paymentModel = SUBSCRIPTION` if it wasn't already (first-time selection case, §9).

### 4.3 Wallet recharge flow

As in §3.3 above; mechanically identical to the original design's recharge flow (`plan.md` "Wallet Recharge Flow"), with the addition of predefined-option validation.

### 4.4 Ride eligibility flow

```text
Driver requests/receives ride
        ↓
Determine driver.paymentModel (read, no lock needed — a classification read, not a financial mutation)
        ↓
       ┌────────────┴────────────┐
       ▼                         ▼
  SUBSCRIPTION               COMMISSION
       │                         │
  subscriptionRepo          commissionPlanService
  .findActive(driverId)     .estimateCommission(ride)
       │                         │
  ACTIVE & unexpired?       wallet.balance >= expectedCommission?
   /        \                  /            \
 YES         NO              YES            NO
  │           │                │              │
  ▼           ▼                ▼              ▼
Allow      Reject           Allow          Reject
(no wallet  (409            (no             (409
 touch)      SUBSCRIPTION    reservation,     INSUFFICIENT_
             _REQUIRED)      no lock)         COMMISSION_BALANCE)
```

Both branches record which model governed this specific ride, on the `Ride` row itself, at the moment of acceptance (see §6, §9) — this is the mechanism that prevents "one ride charged by two different models."

### 4.5 Ride completion flow

```text
Ride completed
        ↓
Read the ride's PINNED payment model (from the Ride row, set at accept time — NOT the driver's current, possibly-since-changed model)
        ↓
       ┌────────────┴────────────┐
       ▼                         ▼
  SUBSCRIPTION               COMMISSION
       │                         │
  No wallet check.          Calculate final commission (existing PricingService output, unchanged)
  No wallet transaction.         │
  No commission ledger      Lock wallet row (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE)
  entry.                         │
       │                    deducted = min(finalCommission, currentBalance)
       │                         │
       │                    Write immutable wallet transaction (idempotent — see §10)
       │                         │
       │                    Post balanced ledger group (DRIVER_COMMISSION_WALLET debit / PLATFORM_COMMISSION credit)
       ▼                         ▼
       Record normal ride/payment information (existing, unchanged for both branches)
```

### 4.6 Commission deduction flow

Detailed in §9 below.

### 4.7 Payment confirmation flow

Unchanged from the original design for both subscription and recharge — both are `PaymentIntent`s distinguished by `purpose`, confirmed exclusively via provider webhook/confirm-call, never by client claim (`plan.md` "Payment Webhook / Idempotency", unaffected by this revision).

## 5. Updated State Machines

### 5.1 Subscription states

**Unchanged** from the original design (`data-model.md §6`): `PENDING_PAYMENT → ACTIVE → EXPIRED`, `ACTIVE → CANCELLED`, `FAILED` terminal-with-retry. One addition: entering `ACTIVE` for the first time also sets `Driver.paymentModel = SUBSCRIPTION` if not already set (§9).

### 5.2 Wallet / recharge states

**Unchanged**: `PaymentIntent` state machine (`CREATED → PENDING/PROCESSING → SUCCEEDED/FAILED/CANCELLED`, etc., `research.md §1.2`, from the pre-existing `IntentService`) governs recharge exactly as it governs subscription payment and customer topup — reused, not modified.

### 5.3 Ride / commission states (revised)

```text
Ride ACCEPTED
   │  (driverPaymentModel snapshot written: 'SUBSCRIPTION' | 'COMMISSION')
   ▼
Ride IN_PROGRESS ─── (subscription may expire here; no effect, BD-2)
   ▼
Ride COMPLETED
   │
   ├── driverPaymentModel = SUBSCRIPTION → no commission sub-state; ride payment info recorded as today
   │
   └── driverPaymentModel = COMMISSION → CommissionDeduction:
          NOT_YET_PROCESSED ──(deduct, idempotent, exactly once)──► PROCESSED
          (a retried/duplicate completion trigger finds PROCESSED and no-ops — see §10)
```

There is no reservation sub-state (`RESERVED → RELEASED/CONSUMED`) in this revision — removed entirely.

### 5.4 Payment states

**Unchanged** (`PaymentIntent`/`PaymentTransaction` states, from `research.md §1.2`) — this revision does not touch payment-provider-facing state machines at all.

## 6. Database Changes

Full detail in the updated `data-model.md`; summarized here with **reason for every change**, per the instruction.

### Keep (unchanged from original design)

- `SubscriptionPlan`, `DriverSubscription` — reason: subscription mechanics are unaffected by the dual-model split; only `billingPeriod`'s value set gains `DAILY`.
- `PaymentIntent.purpose` additive column — reason: still the correct, minimal way to distinguish subscription/recharge/customer-topup intents; unaffected by reservation removal.
- Two new ledger accounts (`DRIVER_COMMISSION_WALLET`, `SUBSCRIPTION_REVENUE`) — reason: still the correct accounting treatment; unaffected.
- `driver_commission_wallet_transactions` table itself — reason: still needed for `MANUAL_RECHARGE`/`RIDE_COMMISSION`/`REFUND`/`ADMIN_CREDIT`/`ADMIN_DEBIT`.

### Modify

- `DriverCommissionWallet` — **remove** `lockedBalance` column. Reason: no reservation exists to lock; a column that can never be written is worse than no column (an implementer would otherwise wonder what sets it). Since this was never deployed, this is a pre-implementation design correction, not a live migration dropping real data.
- `DriverCommissionWalletTransaction.txnType` vocabulary — **remove** `COMMISSION_RESERVATION` and `RESERVATION_RELEASE`. Reason: no reservation events occur.
- `DriverCommissionWalletTransaction.expectedAmount` — **keep, meaning changes**: was "the pre-cap amount when capped against the _reservation_"; now "the pre-cap amount when capped against _available balance at completion_." Reason: same field, same purpose (visibility into under-collection), the reference point it's compared against changed.

### Add

- `Driver.paymentModel` (String, nullable until first selection: `SUBSCRIPTION` | `COMMISSION`) — reason: the backend must have one authoritative place recording which model currently applies to a driver, read by the ride-eligibility flow; placed on `Driver` because it is a driver-classification attribute read the same way `verificationStatus`/`isSuspended` already are, not a financial-transaction record itself (constitution §1.4's "module owns the mutation of its domain" is satisfied because `drivers` already owns `Driver` row mutation).
- `Driver.pendingPaymentModel` (String?, nullable) — reason: a staged model-change (§9) needs somewhere to record "will become X," mirroring `DriverSubscription.pendingPlanId`'s existing staged-change pattern (BD-3).
- `Ride.driverPaymentModel` (String, set once at acceptance, never updated afterward) — reason: this is the mechanism that guarantees "never charge one ride under two models," per the explicit instruction; ride completion reads this snapshot, never the driver's live, possibly-since-changed `paymentModel`.
- `SubscriptionPlan.billingPeriod` — value set extended to include `'DAILY'` — reason: "1-Day" plan requirement; no schema change (still a String column), only a new accepted value, validated at the application layer.
- `CommissionPlan` (new table: `id`, `name`, `status`, `commissionType` [`PERCENTAGE`|`FLAT`], `commissionValue`, `currency`, timestamps) — reason: the instruction explicitly requires distinguishing a "Commission-Based Plan... defines how commission is calculated" from the Subscription Plan and the Wallet. **Scope note** (not silently assumed — flagged per instruction): for this revision, exactly one system-default `CommissionPlan` is assumed active at a time and its `commissionValue` is a label/record of the rate the existing `PricingService` already applies — this table does **not**, in this revision, replace `PricingService`'s actual fare/commission math (which the original spec's Assumptions section already committed to reusing, not reinventing) or introduce genuinely per-driver differentiated commission rates. If per-driver or multi-tier commission rates that actually change the calculated amount are wanted, that is additional scope requiring its own confirmation — see the Approval Checklist.
- `WalletRechargeOption` (new table: `id`, `amount`, `label`, `status`, `sortOrder`, timestamps) — reason: "predefined recharge amount/box" must be company-configurable, per the instruction's explicit requirement; a driver's custom-amount entry is validated against configured min/max (new config knobs, no table needed for that half).

### No longer required (removed from the design)

- Reuse of `wallet_holds` for commission reservation — reason: no reservation exists. `wallet_holds` itself is untouched (still exists for the customer wallet, exactly as originally built — nothing here deletes or alters that table).
- Partial unique index `wallet_hold_one_active_per_reference` (commission-scoped) — reason: nothing to constrain, since `wallet_holds` is no longer used by this feature at all.
- `DRIVER_COMMISSION_WALLET_RESERVATION_TTL_SEC` config knob and its safety-net sweep job — reason: nothing can be orphaned if nothing is ever reserved.

## 7. API Changes

| API                                                         | Change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `POST /api/v1/drivers/payment-model`                        | **New.** Body `{ paymentModel }`. First selection applies immediately; a change from an existing model is staged per §9 and the response indicates `effectiveAt`. Auth: the authenticated driver (self only). Idempotency: not required (it's a state-setting request, not a payment; re-submitting the same target model is naturally idempotent — a repeat call with the same value is a no-op). ## 9. Ride Commission Flow

**Expected commission** (accept time, commission-model only): `commissionPlanService.estimateCommission(ride)` — reads the active `CommissionPlan` and the ride's estimated fare (via the existing `PricingService` estimate, unchanged source), returns an amount. Pure read, no side effect.

**Wallet eligibility** (accept time, commission-model only): a single comparison, `wallet.balance >= expectedCommission`. No lock is strictly required for this read (nothing is being reserved), though the implementation may still perform it inside the existing `acceptRideRequest` transaction for simplicity/consistency with the rest of that method — reading inside a transaction does not imply reserving. If the check passes, `Ride.driverPaymentModel = 'COMMISSION'` is written as part of the same `rideRepo.create(...)` call that already exists in `acceptRideRequest` (an additional column value on an insert that already happens — not a new write).

**Ride completion** (commission-model only, i.e. `ride.driverPaymentModel === 'COMMISSION'`): final commission computed exactly as before (`itemizedFare.platformCommission`, unchanged source). The wallet row is locked (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) — this is the **only** lock in the whole flow, and it exists for ordinary write-safety (never read a stale balance mid-write), not to resolve a reservation race.

**Final commission** vs. **wallet deduction**:

```text
lockedWallet = commissionWalletRepository.lockForUpdate(driverId, tx)
deducted = min(finalCommission, lockedWallet.balance)
newBalance = lockedWallet.balance - deducted
```

If `deducted < finalCommission` (wallet balance was already insufficient by completion time — e.g. an admin debit occurred between accept and completion, or the fare came in higher than the accept-time estimate), the shortfall (`finalCommission - deducted`) is written to `expectedAmount` for reporting (BD-1, revised), and `newBalance` is never allowed below zero through this path.

**Wallet transaction**: one `DriverCommissionWalletTransaction` row, `txnType: 'RIDE_COMMISSION'`, `amount: -deducted`, `balanceAfter: newBalance`, `referenceType: 'RIDE'`, `referenceId: ride.id`.

**Ledger entry**: one balanced group — `DRIVER_COMMISSION_WALLET` DEBIT `deducted` / `PLATFORM_COMMISSION` CREDIT `deducted` — posted via the existing `LedgerService.postTransactionGroup`, in the **same transaction** as the wallet write and the ride's status transition to `COMPLETED` (constitution §4.1/§4.3, unchanged requirement).

**Payment-model switching and single-model-per-ride** (new, detailed): a driver's `paymentModel` can change between when a ride is accepted and when it completes (e.g. they buy a subscription mid-ride). Because ride completion reads `Ride.driverPaymentModel` — the value **pinned at accept time** — rather than `Driver.paymentModel`'s current, possibly-changed value, a ride that started as `COMMISSION` always completes as `COMMISSION` (wallet deducted) and a ride that started as `SUBSCRIPTION` always completes as `SUBSCRIPTION` (nothing deducted), regardless of any switch in between. This is the direct implementation of "do not allow one ride to be charged using two different models" and "the payment model applicable to the ride must be determined consistently by the backend."

**When a model switch takes effect for _future_ rides**:

- `SUBSCRIPTION → COMMISSION`: if the driver has a currently `ACTIVE` subscription, the switch is **staged** (`Driver.pendingPaymentModel = 'COMMISSION'`) and applied by the same expiry sweep that already handles subscription expiry — at the current period's `expiryDate`, mirroring BD-3's "no proration, current paid period runs its course" exactly, generalized from "plan change" to "model change." If the driver has **no** active subscription (e.g. it already lapsed), the switch applies immediately — there is no paid access being wasted.
- `COMMISSION → SUBSCRIPTION`: applies **immediately upon subscription purchase and activation** — there is no time-boxed "already-paid access" on the commission side to preserve (a wallet balance isn't a period; it just sits, unspent, ready for later use if the driver ever switches back). The driver's very next ride offer is evaluated under `SUBSCRIPTION`; any ride **already accepted** before the switch still completes under `COMMISSION` per the pinning rule above.

## 10. Concurrency and Transaction Safety

**Why reservation/freeze is not required under the one-active-ride-per-driver rule** (the instruction explicitly asks this to be explained clearly):

1. **The scenario reservation existed to prevent** was: driver has ₹200 available; two different ride requests, each needing ₹150 commission, are both accepted by the same driver at nearly the same instant; without a lock, both accept-time checks could read "₹200 available, ₹150 needed, OK" before either commits, and both would proceed — over-committing the wallet before either ride even starts. Reservation solved this by having the _first_ accept immediately earmark ₹150 as unavailable to the second.

2. **But this platform already has a separate, pre-existing rule that a driver can have at most one active ride at a time** (`rideRepo.findActiveByDriver`, checked in `acceptRideRequest` before any ride is created — `research.md §1.8`, step 4, unrelated to this feature and unmodified by it). This means: by the time a _second_ ride's accept-time check could matter, the _first_ ride must already have failed to become the driver's active ride, or the second accept must itself be rejected by the existing no-active-ride guard. **There is no business scenario, under this platform's existing rules, where a driver legitimately has two rides simultaneously consuming commission.** Reservation was solving a race for a situation the platform's own ride-assignment rules already make structurally impossible in the steady state.

3. **The only place money actually moves is ride completion, and completion happens once per ride, guarded by existing idempotency machinery.** Because at most one ride is ever "in flight" for a given driver, at most one completion-triggered deduction is ever pending for that driver's wallet at a time under normal operation. The lock taken at deduction time (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on the wallet row) is there for the ordinary reason every write in this codebase locks the row it's about to read-modify-write (constitution §5.1) — not to arbitrate between competing simultaneous consumers, because there structurally aren't any.

4. **Defense in depth, without inventing a user-facing frozen balance**: even in a hypothetical scenario where the platform's own one-active-ride invariant had a bug and somehow let two rides be simultaneously active for one driver (a pre-existing concern entirely outside this feature's scope to fix), the wallet-side design here still cannot be corrupted: each completion locks the row, reads the **current** balance (not a value cached from accept time), and caps its own deduction at whatever is actually there. The second ride to complete would simply find a smaller (possibly zero) balance and cap accordingly — exactly the same BD-1 "never negative" policy already in place, applied per-completion rather than per-reservation. **The database transaction/row lock at the time of deduction is the actual correctness boundary; reservation would only have made the common case slightly more explicit at the cost of a whole extra state (locked balance) that a "one active ride" system doesn't need.**

5. **Duplicate/retried completion for the _same_ ride** — an orthogonal concern reservation never addressed anyway — is still handled exactly as in the original design: a partial unique index on `(ride_id) WHERE txn_type = 'RIDE_COMMISSION'` guarantees at most one deduction per ride ever exists, and the deduction logic checks for an existing row before writing a new one (idempotent by construction, unchanged from the original design's FR-021, `data-model.md §4`).

**Net effect**: removing reservation does not reduce correctness. It removes a mechanism that was defending against a race this platform's ride-assignment rules already prevent, while keeping the one mechanism (row lock at the point of money movement) that was ever actually load-bearing.

## 11. Accounting / Ledger Flow

**Unchanged from the original design in mechanism** (`data-model.md §5`, reused): `LedgerService.postTransactionGroup`, two accounts (`DRIVER_COMMISSION_WALLET`, `SUBSCRIPTION_REVENUE`), existing accounts reused (`GATEWAY_CLEARING`, `PLATFORM_COMMISSION`). The instruction's explicit distinctions are already satisfied by this design and are restated here for completeness, since they're worth being explicit about:

- **Subscription revenue** ≠ wallet recharge: a subscription payment posts `GATEWAY_CLEARING` debit / `SUBSCRIPTION_REVENUE` credit — recognized as revenue **immediately**, once, at payment time. A wallet recharge posts `GATEWAY_CLEARING` debit / `DRIVER_COMMISSION_WALLET` credit — **not** revenue yet, a liability/earmark, exactly like `CUSTOMER_WALLET`'s existing treatment. These are different accounts, posted by different flows, and one is never mistaken for the other.
- **Ride commission** ≠ wallet recharge: a commission deduction posts `DRIVER_COMMISSION_WALLET` debit / `PLATFORM_COMMISSION` credit — this is the moment revenue is actually recognized _from_ the earmarked wallet liability, per ride. It only ever fires for `COMMISSION`-model rides; it never fires as a side effect of a recharge.
- **Customer ride payment** ≠ wallet recharge: entirely separate ledger legs (`CUSTOMER_WALLET`/`GATEWAY_CLEARING` against `DRIVER_PAYABLE`/`TAX_PAYABLE`/`PLATFORM_FEE`/`PLATFORM_COMMISSION`, via `recordTripPayment`, unchanged) — no code path in either flow reads or writes the other's accounts, confirmed unchanged by `research.md §1.6`.
- **Driver earnings/payout** ≠ any of the above: `RideFare.driverEarning` and `SettlementService`/`PayoutService` are completely untouched by this feature, exactly as in the original design.

Every journal still balances (constitution §4.3): recharge, subscription payment, and commission deduction are each a single matched debit/credit pair, unchanged in structure from the original design — only the deduction's _amount_ computation changed (capped against current balance, not a reservation).

## 15. Final Acceptance Criteria

1. A driver has exactly one `paymentModel` at any given time once selected; before selection, the driver is ineligible for any ride under either model.
2. A `SUBSCRIPTION`-model driver's ride eligibility depends solely on having an active, unexpired subscription; their completed rides never produce a Commission Wallet transaction or a commission ledger entry.
3. A `COMMISSION`-model driver's ride eligibility depends solely on `wallet.balance >= expectedCommission` at accept time, computed server-side, with no locking, freezing, or reservation of any kind.
4. A `COMMISSION`-model driver never has a subscription requirement.
5. Every completed `COMMISSION`-model ride deducts `min(finalCommission, currentBalance)` from the wallet exactly once, atomically with a balanced ledger entry, and the wallet never goes negative through this path.
6. A ride's payment-model handling is fixed at acceptance and is never affected by a later change to the driver's `paymentModel` — verified by the pinned-model tests in §14.
7. Wallet recharge amounts are limited to a company-configured predefined list or a company-configured custom range, validated server-side.
8. Wallet crediting and subscription activation both occur exclusively on payment-provider confirmation, never on client claim, and exactly once regardless of duplicate confirmations/requests.
9. Customer ride payment remains fully isolated from every driver-side flow this feature touches.
10. No reservation, hold, or locked-balance concept exists anywhere in the shipped design.
