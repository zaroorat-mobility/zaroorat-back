# Vision

**Owner:** Founders · **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-06

---

## Mission

> **Make reliable, fairly-priced transport a tap away for everyone — and a dignified income
> for every driver who partners with us.**

"Zaroorat" (ضرورت) means _need_. The product is named for the everyday need to get somewhere,
and the driver's need to earn. Both sides of that promise are first-class; neither is an
afterthought.

## The problem

In our launch markets, urban mobility is unreliable and opaque:

- **Riders** face inconsistent availability, opaque and negotiated fares, safety uncertainty,
  and few cashless options. Booking a bike or rickshaw often means haggling on the street.
- **Drivers** face high commissions from incumbents, unpredictable earnings, delayed payouts,
  and little support when something goes wrong.
- **The market** is large, young, mobile-first, and cash-heavy — a good fit for a lean,
  trust-building ride-hailing product that treats drivers well.

## What we are building

A ride-hailing platform connecting riders and drivers across bikes, rickshaws, and economy
cars, with:

- **Transparent, up-front pricing** — the rider sees the fare before booking; no haggling.
- **Fast, fair matching** — nearest suitable driver, with rules that spread earnings.
- **Cash + wallet** — meeting a cash-first market where it is, with a path to cashless.
- **Safety built in** — verified drivers, trip sharing, in-app support, SOS.
- **Driver-first economics** — competitive commission, fast payouts, transparent earnings.

## Positioning

> For **budget-conscious urban riders** who need dependable everyday transport, Zaroorat Ride
> is a **ride-hailing app** that offers **transparent fares and verified drivers** — unlike
> street-hailing and incumbents, we combine **fair rider pricing with driver-first economics**.

We do **not** compete on being the cheapest at any cost, nor on luxury. We compete on
**trust and reliability for both sides**, sustainably priced.

## North-star metric

> **Weekly Completed Trips** — the number of trips completed per week.

It is the single number that only goes up when we're genuinely serving both sides: riders find
value (they book), drivers are available and accept (supply), matching works (they connect),
and the experience is good enough to complete and repeat. Vanity metrics (downloads, signups)
are explicitly _not_ our north star.

### Supporting metrics (the health of the north star)

| Dimension    | Metric                                                                |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rider value  | Weekly active riders, trips/rider/week, rider retention (W4)          |
| Driver value | Weekly active drivers, earnings/online-hour, driver retention (W4)    |
| Marketplace  | Request→match rate, match→completion rate, avg pickup ETA             |
| Trust        | Trip completion rate, cancellation rate, avg rating, safety incidents |
| Business     | Take rate, contribution margin per trip, CAC payback                  |

Definitions and how each is computed live in [05_monetization-and-metrics.md](05_monetization-and-metrics.md).

## Non-goals (for now)

Stating what we will **not** do keeps focus:

- ❌ Food/parcel delivery, freight, intercity — later, if at all.
- ❌ Owning a fleet or employing drivers — we are an aggregator (see assumption A5).
- ❌ Premium/luxury tiers at launch.
- ❌ Expansion beyond launch cities before unit economics are proven.

## Guiding principles

1. **Both sides win, or neither does.** Every feature is evaluated for rider _and_ driver impact.
2. **Trust is the product.** Safety and transparency beat a marginally lower price.
3. **Cash is not a bug.** We meet the market where it is and earn the move to cashless.
4. **Fast to book, fast to earn.** Latency in matching is lost trips and lost income.
5. **Sustainable, not subsidized.** Growth that only works with permanent subsidy isn't growth.
